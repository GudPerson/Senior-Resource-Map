# Organisation verification procedure

Status: Gate 1 operating procedure; fictional rehearsal may proceed
Applies before approving any real organisation or first Organisation Admin

## Separation of duties

The applicant supplies the registration request. A CareAround Super Admin
performs the independent verification and records the decision. The applicant's
submitted website, email address or logo is evidence to check, not independent
proof by itself.

Pending written employment guidance, the product owner must not recruit
providers assigned through their AIC role or use AIC contacts, confidential
information, systems or authority for CareAround onboarding. Fictional
rehearsals remain permitted.

## Approval checklist

1. Confirm the organisation's legal or operating name and public contact using
   a source obtained independently of the submitted request.
2. Contact the organisation through that independently obtained channel and
   confirm that the named applicant is authorised to register the organisation.
3. Confirm control of the exact submitted email domain. Do not accept consumer
   email domains, suffix matches, lookalike domains or redirects to a different
   registrant.
4. Confirm the first proposed Organisation Admin's name, role and organisation
   email through the verified contact.
5. Review the exact Terms version accepted by the applicant. Confirm that the
   organisation grants CareAround the stated uses of its supplied logo, banner
   and content and understands how to revoke that grant.
6. Inspect the supplied HTTPS website, logo and banner locations. Reject
   unrelated, inaccessible, unsafe or third-party assets that the organisation
   has not confirmed it controls or may license.
7. Record approval only after every check passes. Use a specific internal reason
   when rejecting or requesting correction; do not store unnecessary identity
   documents or private correspondence in the public resource record.
8. Approve the first Organisation Admin as a separate action. Verify that no
   session is issued before approval and that the account receives access only
   to the approved organisation.

## Evidence record

Keep the minimum operational evidence needed to explain the decision:

- request and organisation identifiers;
- verifier and decision timestamps;
- verification channel and public source type;
- confirmed domain and authorised-representative outcome;
- accepted Terms version and digital-asset grant state;
- decision reason; and
- any expiry, revalidation date or revocation.

Do not place passwords, authentication tokens, full private correspondence,
unnecessary identity documents or secret values in the audit metadata.

## Revalidation and revocation

Reverify when the organisation changes domain, administrator, legal identity,
asset ownership or agreement version. Suspend new access decisions when control
is disputed. Revoking an organisation, agreement or asset pack must prevent
future approved public use without deleting the historical audit record.
